# Stead

## Register

product

## Users

People managing household and personal responsibilities, often coordinating everyday tasks, purchases, finances, schedules, and shared information with other household members. They need fast, low-friction workflows that remain clear during frequent routine use.

## Product Purpose

Stead brings household and personal management into one dependable workspace. It helps people see what needs attention, coordinate ownership, and complete routine work without losing context. Success means everyday administration feels calm, obvious, and trustworthy.

## Brand Personality

Quiet, refined, dependable. The product should feel composed and capable, with concise language and feedback that reassures without demanding attention.

## Anti-references

Avoid flashy, playful, or novelty-driven interfaces. Avoid excessive motion, celebratory effects, visual noise, and interaction patterns that make routine household work feel performative or distracting.

## Design Principles

1. Keep routine actions immediate and easy to understand.
2. Preserve context when state changes, especially when items move between workflows.
3. Confirm meaningful actions with calm, concise feedback.
4. Make shared ownership and status visible without adding clutter.
5. Prefer dependable behavior and recoverable errors over decorative flourish.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Support keyboard navigation, clear focus states, sufficient contrast, semantic status announcements, and system reduced-motion preferences. State changes must not rely on color or animation alone.
